🦷 乳牙功能與成長里程碑

❌ 常見誤解：「乳牙遲早會換掉，蛀掉也沒關係」

🍽️

咀嚼與營養吸收

充分咀嚼幫助消化，保障健康發育

🗣️

說話與正確發音

輔助齒音（s、z、t）精準發音

🧭

為恆牙預留空間

乳牙是恆牙「導航員」，維持牙弓空間

😊

自信社交與優質睡眠

健康笑容帶來自信，無痛好眠

⚠️ 嚴重警訊：乳牙嚴重蛀牙會引發劇烈疼痛與牙齦腫脹，甚至感染底下的「恆牙胚」，對未來恆牙造成不可逆的永久損害！

🪥 含氟牙膏使用指引

氟化物是保護兒童牙齒的「修補與加固材料」。每日刷牙兩次，家長全程監督，教導吐出泡沫，避免吞食。

📏 香港參考用量（按年齡）：
6個月至2歲
一粒米大小
2歲至6歲
青豆大小
6歲或以上
約牙刷頭1/3

💡 不同孩子蛀牙風險各異，如有疑問請諮詢牙醫。

📋 專案管理思維：六個月習慣養成

化解「刷牙大戰」不能單靠懲罰或強迫，運用五大步驟：

🔔 提示 固定時間與地點（早餐後、睡前），建立條件反射
🔄 常規 家長示範，孩子先自己刷，滿足自主感
👀 監督與補刷 7-8歲前每晚由家長「最後補刷」
⭐ 正向獎勵 打卡集點貼紙，切忌用甜食獎勵
📅 定期跟進 每半年檢查、塗氟及六歲臼齒窩溝封閉

🪥 如何挑選合適的刷牙工具？

三個核心提問：

  • ✅ 手柄大小合用嗎？適合兒童小手抓握、防滑厚實握柄
  • ✅ 刷頭細小、軟毛？刷頭約2顆牙齒長度，極軟毛保護牙齦
  • ✅ 孩子會天天開心使用嗎？手動/電動/智能App皆可，關鍵是刷足2分鐘時間＋家長補刷

🦸 打造無懼體驗：正向看牙溝通原則

✨ 首次看牙黃金時機：一歲前後或第一顆乳牙萌出時

👍 建議做的正向溝通

  • 用繪本、卡通、扮家家酒介紹診所
  • 「牙醫會數數牙齒、幫牙齒洗澡喔！」
  • 將牙醫塑造為「保護牙齒的超級英雄」

🚫 應避免的錯誤言語

  • 絕不威脅：「不聽話就帶你去打針！」
  • 避免負面暗示：「不會痛的/不要害怕」
  • 不在孩子面前分享看牙焦慮經驗

✅ 家長日常護齒實操清單

  • ☐ 起床後：使用適齡含氟牙膏刷牙2分鐘，吐出泡沫
  • ☐ 睡前洗漱：孩子先自己刷 ➔ 家長最後徹底補刷（重點：後排臼齒與齒縫）
  • ☐ 工具檢視：牙刷刷毛開叉立即更換（建議每2-3個月更換）

🦷 Primary Teeth Functions & Developmental Milestones

❌ Common Misconception: "Primary teeth will eventually fall out anyway, so cavities don't matter"

🍽️

Chewing & Nutrient Absorption

Thorough chewing helps with digestion and ensures healthy physical development

🗣️

Speech & Clear Pronunciation

Helps children speak clearly and pronounce words correctly (like s, z, and t sounds)

🧭

Space Reservation for Adult Teeth

Primary teeth act as guides for adult teeth, keeping space and preventing crowding

😊

Confidence & Quality Sleep

A healthy smile boosts confidence and ensures good sleep without tooth pain

⚠️ Serious Warning: Severe decay in primary teeth can cause intense pain and swollen gums, and may damage the adult teeth growing underneath — causing permanent damage that cannot be fixed!

🪥 Fluoride Toothpaste Usage Guide

Fluoride is an effective protective coating that helps repair and strengthen children's teeth. Brush twice daily — parents must supervise and teach children to spit out the foam, avoiding swallowing.

📏 Hong Kong Reference Dosage (By Age):
6 months to 2 years
A grain of rice size
2 to 6 years
A pea-sized amount
6 years and above
About 1/3 of brush head

💡 Every child has different cavity risk levels. If unsure which amount to use, ask your dentist.

📋 Smart Habit Building: Six-Month Plan for Good Brushing Habits

Getting children to brush doesn't have to be a battle! Use five simple steps:

🔔 Set a Trigger Pick the same time and place every day (after breakfast, before bed) so brushing becomes automatic
🔄 Make It a Routine Show your child the correct way first, then let them brush by themselves to feel independent
👀 Check & Help Finish Before age 7-8, parents should do a thorough final brushing every night
⭐ Give Positive Rewards Use sticker charts or a reward system — don't use candy as rewards
📅 Regular Check-ups Visit the dentist every six months for cleaning, fluoride treatment, and protective coatings

🪥 How to Choose the Right Toothbrush for Your Child

Three Important Questions:

  • ✅ Is the handle the right size? Choose a toothbrush with a thick, easy-to-grip handle designed for small children's hands
  • ✅ Are the bristles small and soft? The brush head should be about the size of 2 teeth; bristles must be very soft to protect tender gums
  • ✅ Will your child actually want to use it every day? Regular, electric, or smart app toothbrushes all work — what matters most is brushing for 2 minutes fully + parent helping finish

🦸 Making Dental Visits Easy & Fun: Positive Communication Tips

✨ Best Time for First Dental Visit: Around one year old or when the first baby tooth comes in

👍 What You Should Say (DOs)

  • Use books, cartoons, or pretend play to introduce the dental office
  • "The dentist will count your teeth and give them a nice wash!"
  • Tell your child: "The dentist is your friend and superhero who takes care of teeth"

🚫 What You Shouldn't Say (DON'Ts)

  • Never threaten: "If you don't listen, I'll take you to the dentist for a shot!"
  • Don't mention scary things — saying "It won't hurt" makes kids more nervous
  • Don't share your own dental fears with your child

✅ Parents' Daily Tooth Care Checklist

  • ☐ When your child wakes up: Use age-appropriate fluoride toothpaste and brush for 2 minutes fully, then spit out the foam
  • ☐ Before bedtime: Let your child brush first ➔ Then you do a thorough final brushing (pay special attention to back teeth and between teeth)
  • ☐ Check the toothbrush: Replace the toothbrush right away if bristles are bent or damaged (change toothbrush every 2-3 months)
